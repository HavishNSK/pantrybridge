# FullPlate review priorities

- Every required ingredient, including staples and zero-price water, needs confirmed stock or an explicitly confirmed resource. Never infer availability from rounded grocery costs.
- Pantry stock and additional budgets must be allocated across all meals; preserve non-target plan entries and account for displaced leftover sources. Optional ingredients cannot consume stock required later.
- Validate review quantities and persisted cooking preferences. Sample-derived inventory must retain demo provenance through edits and merges.
- Photo references remain local and temporary. Never claim image recognition, receipt OCR, eligibility checks, food-safety assessment, medical accuracy, or measured savings unless implemented and substantiated.
- Keep Add food → Find dinners → Save simple. Preserve error focus, keyboard access, explicit replacement confirmation, deletion/reset, and access to manually added shopping items when the week is empty.
- Verify regressions with relevant Vitest/Playwright checks; distinguish measured scoped coverage from whole-app coverage. Credentials, private photos, and browser storage exports must not enter source or review artifacts.
